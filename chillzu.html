Wellness · React · User Research

Product

A wellness app designed to help teenagers build healthier habits.

My role

Founder. I researched the problem, designed the experience, built the product in React/JavaScript, and tested it with users.

What I learned

User testing changed what I built and taught me to start with what people actually need, not what I assume they need.

Today

That interest in technology and well-being continues in my Product Operations work at Flourish Science ↗, where I work directly with user feedback, product issues, and the product team.

From Chillzu to now

What I carried forward

01

Build with users, not just for them. Chillzu was the first time I watched user feedback change something I had already designed and built. It made talking to users part of how I build, not something I do after.

02

I found the part of building I liked most. I enjoyed coding Chillzu, but I was more interested in figuring out what people needed, why something wasn’t working for them, and what we should build next. That’s what pulled me toward product.

03

I came back to well-being technology. Today, I work in Product Operations at Flourish Science ↗, where I triage user feedback, investigate product issues, and turn what users are experiencing into actionable work for the product team.
